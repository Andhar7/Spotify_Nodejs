  1. Ethereal Email (Recommended - Easiest)

  - Completely free
  - No signup required
  - Auto-generates test credentials
  - Works perfectly with Nodemailer

  2. Gmail with App Password

  - Free if you have a Gmail account
  - Good for testing real email delivery
  - Requires 2FA and app-specific password

  3. Resend (Modern alternative)

  - Free tier: 3,000 emails/month
  - Simple API
  - Good for both testing and production

  4. Brevo (formerly Sendinblue)

  - Free tier: 300 emails/day
  - Real email delivery
